PRML (Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning by Christopher Bishop) book is designed to address the exact need of practical machine learning without compromising with the rigorous mathematical foundations and no working implementation to avoid distraction from the inner beauty. This feature becomes a limitation for students, practicing professionals and scientists alike who would like to play with it using computer programs..

This book is an attempt to decode the myth and mystery of the nucleus of one of the most fascinating books of core machine learning algorithms, PRML by Christopher Bishop (2009), in its full glory, delving bit‐by‐bit from a hacker's perspective.

There are several books on machine learning, but (sadly) only The PRML book illustrates a real mathematical approach. But the rigor and complexity of this alone book makes it inaccessible to all but the most determined, dedicated and long‐suffering student. This has created a huge gap between theory and practice, which often leads to obscure understanding.

While I set myself on the book, I started making notes, adding illustrations, sprinkling comments, implementing algorithms in C++ and Python to unravel the inner beauty Bishop set down. My curiosity, wearing a hacker's hat, mirroring the shape of the algorithms with code, fulfilling the unquenchable thirst, started settling down over the period in a form of this book, a supplement to the real core.

To allow myself to drift along with the core vision, I adopted to use LaTeX, a documentation preparation system, which facilitates the creation of readable illustrations in sync with beautiful diagrams and programs.

Needless to say, this work aims to illuminate what happens beneath the abstraction layers, not through dry specifications, but by walking through actual, running code; following the tradition of John Lion's legendary Commentary on UNIX.

This is not a replacement for PRML’s book. You should own and study the original. Think of this as a hacker sitting beside you, pointing at the screen, saying: See this line? Here’s why it matters...

This book provides a conceptual approach to reading and solving problems in the PRML book. Generalized methods, principles and techniques are discussed to foster a deeper understanding of elementary machine learning. Primary emphasis is on the fundamental principles underlying all topics, thus enabling the reader to solve any problem after mastering this book in its entirety.

The present work is will serve as a complete guide to private students reading the subject with few or no opportunities of instruction. This will save time and lighten the work of Teachers as well. This book helps in acquiring a better understanding of the basic principles of elementary machine learning and in revising a large amount of the subject matter quickly.

This book provides conceptual solutions to all the exercises and problems along with additional ones throughout the book to demonstrate the general methodological principles in play. This leads to generalization and simplification of problem‐solving paradigm and enforces correctness as a part of the design of solutions with great emphasis on foundational science of elementary mathematics. Though aimed primarily at students, it imparts the knowledge of deep internals of underlying fundamental concepts to teachers alike.

Care has been taken, as in the forthcoming ones, to present the concepts and solutions with multi‐concepts and beyond in a simple natural manner, in order to meet the difficulties which are most likely to arise, and to render the work intelligible and instructive.

This work contains several variations of problems, solutions, methods, approaches to enrich, strengthen and enliven the inherent multi‐concepts.
